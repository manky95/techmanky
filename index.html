FAQs

Questions patients ask us

Where are your clinics located?

Camp clinic: 2158, Shop No 5, Mayfair Society, Jaan Mohammad Road, New Modikhana, Pune – 411001 (near Hotel Goodluck).
Koregaon Park Annexe clinic: Vanshaj Society, Pingale Wasti, opposite the old Passport Office, Mundhwa, Pune – 411036.

Is there a clinic near Koregaon Park, Mundhwa or Kharadi?

Yes — our newly opened Koregaon Park Annexe clinic in Mundhwa (opposite the old Passport Office) is just minutes from Koregaon Park, Keshav Nagar and Kharadi. Get directions →

What are the clinic timings?

Both clinics are open Monday to Saturday, 10 am – 9 pm. Sundays are reserved for pre-booked appointments. Evening slots fill quickly, so call +91 98601 56435 or WhatsApp us to book ahead.

Is root canal treatment painful?

No. With modern anaesthesia and rotary instruments, root canal treatment here is virtually painless — many patients describe it as easier than a filling. Most return to normal routine the same day.

Do you offer clear aligners instead of braces?

Yes. We provide Illusion clear aligners in FLEX and STANDARD plans — removable, nearly invisible trays designed to go maximum years without replacement.

How long does a dental implant take?

Implant placement is usually one sitting; the permanent crown is fitted after a healing period of around 3–6 months. Dr. Rasal will confirm your timeline after an X-ray and consultation.

Do I need an appointment, or can I walk in?

Walk-ins are welcome Monday to Saturday at both clinics, but booking ahead guarantees your slot and reduces waiting time — especially for evenings and weekends.
